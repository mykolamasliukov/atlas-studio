What it is

ATLAS Studio is a video production tool that its owner runs on his own computer. It turns an idea into a finished film: script, frames, clips, voice-over and montage. Its Publishing section then posts the owner's finished videos to his own YouTube channels, Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and TikTok accounts through the platforms' official APIs (YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API, TikTok Content Posting API).

  • Runs locally on the owner's computer; there is no hosted service, no user sign-up and no third-party users.
  • Publishes only to accounts the owner connects himself, only content he chooses, at the time he sets.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time.
  • AI-generated videos are labelled as such where the platform offers a label.
