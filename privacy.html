Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

ATLAS Studio ("the app") is a self-hosted video studio that its owner runs on his own computer to publish his own videos to his own accounts. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the app handles when the owner connects his YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

1. Platforms the app uses

  • YouTube API Services. ATLAS Studio uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram) — through the Meta Graph API; see the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok — through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API; see the TikTok Privacy Policy.

2. What data the app accesses

  • Sign-in (OAuth) tokens issued by the platform when the owner connects an account.
  • Basic account information: channel, Page or account name and ID, profile picture address, and (TikTok) the creator's posting settings such as allowed privacy levels and maximum video length.
  • The videos, titles, descriptions, tags, captions and cover images the owner chooses to publish.
  • The status of each upload (processing, published, errors) and public view, like and comment counts of the owner's published posts.

The app does not access messages, contacts, followers' personal data, payment information or any other data.

3. How the data is used

Only to publish the owner's videos to the accounts he connected, at the time he chooses, and to show him their status and view counts inside the app. Nothing is published without the owner explicitly scheduling it.

4. Where the data is stored and who it is shared with

  • All data stays on the owner's own computer: tokens in a local configuration file, post records and videos in local folders. There is no server operated by ATLAS Studio and no database in the cloud.
  • Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to, directly from the owner's computer, when publishing or checking a post.
  • No data is sold, rented, shared with advertisers or used for analytics, profiling or advertising.

5. Retention

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account in the app (which deletes them) or revokes access at the platform. Post records and copies of videos are kept locally until the owner deletes the post.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google / YouTube: Google security settings — third-party access.
  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → remove ATLAS Studio.
  • TikTok: TikTok → Profile → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions → remove ATLAS Studio.
  • In the app: Publishing → Accounts → Disconnect or Delete.

Step-by-step instructions: Data deletion.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new date.
