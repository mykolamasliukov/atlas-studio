Data deletion instructions

Last updated: 10 October 2026

ATLAS Studio keeps all data on its owner's own computer; it has no server and no cloud database. To delete the data the app holds about your accounts:

In the app

  1. Open ATLAS Studio → Publishing → Accounts.
  2. Press Disconnect (removes the sign-in token and revokes it where the platform allows) or Delete (also removes the account record).
  3. To delete post records and video copies, open each post and press Delete post, or delete the folder work/publish of the studio.

At the platforms

  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → select ATLAS Studio → Remove. Facebook then deletes the app's access.
  • Google / YouTube: Google third-party access → ATLAS Studio → Remove access.
  • TikTok: Profile → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions → ATLAS Studio → Remove access.

Українською

Усі дані — лише на комп'ютері власника. У студії: «Публікація» → «Акаунти» → «Відключити» чи «Видалити»; пости — «Видалити пост». На платформах — прибери доступ ATLAS Studio в налаштуваннях Facebook, Google чи TikTok.
